# Seedance 2.5 — engine sheet for directors

Facts marked **[official]** come from ByteDance/BytePlus documentation (updated 2026-09-28) or platform
API schemas. **[observed]** comes from production prompts and outputs on Higgsfield (a 20-minute
short, Aug–Sep 2026). **[policy]** is this tree's decision where sources are silent or disagree.
Research date: 2026-10-08 — re-check limits when the platform changes.

**Contents:** generation limits · references · audio and speech · multi-shot and timing · negatives ·
prompt structure and length · draft, edit, extend · platform notes · known failure modes

---

## Generation limits

- **Duration:** 4–30 s per generation, whole seconds (or model-chosen) [official]. Production used
  14–30 s generations [observed].
- **Resolution:** 480p, 720p, 1080p (10-bit HEVC); **no 4K on 2.5** [official].
- **Aspect ratios:** 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16, adaptive. 21:9 at 1080p renders 2206×946
  [official, observed]. First-frame, first/last-frame, edit and extend tasks use adaptive [official].
- **Frame rate:** 24 fps [official].
- **[policy] Duration = timeline.** Set the generation's duration to the exact sum of the shot ranges.
  A ~21 s timeline in a 30 s job stretched its last beats (one beat ran ~10 s instead of 4) [observed].

## References

- Up to **30 images, 10 videos, 10 audio clips** (videos and audio each ≤30 s total), **50 references
  max**; request ≤64 MB [official].
- Roles: first frame, last frame, reference image, reference video, reference audio [official].
- What references can control: identity and voice; motion (action, expression, camera, effects); 3D
  clay-model motion and lighting; style; audio (music, dialogue, timbre); storyboards (≤15 line-art
  panels, loose guidance); **keyframes, followed strictly** ("Use Images X to Y in order as
  keyframes."); first/last frame [official].
- Stability: 1–8 image-referenced subjects (9–12 less stable); **more than 4 referenced people is
  unstable**; audio/video subjects 1–5 with 5–10 s clips [official].
- Upload in order of first appearance; describe each mapping in words; bind names to references at
  each mention [official].
- Multi-view character sheets are supported in 2.5 [official].
- Downscale reference images to about the output resolution (oversized ones can print fingerprint
  textures) [official].
- Some providers refuse uploads of real human faces; use generated or authorized portraits [official
  for BytePlus; unverified elsewhere].
- **Production pattern:** keyframe per key shot inside a multi-shot generation ("shot 1 STARTS from
  this exact frame and comes alive from it"); other shots "continue this exact look"; identity
  Elements + turnarounds + props on neutral ground + location plates; previous take as a video
  reference during corrections [observed].

## Audio and speech

- Generates voice, lip-sync, effects and music in the same pass; audio is mono [official].
- **Languages (2.5):** Chinese, English, Spanish, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, Arabic, **Portuguese**,
  Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean [official]. An invented language written in Cyrillic was generated
  convincingly in production [observed].
- Multi-speaker binding ("Images 1–2 are Character 1 and correspond to Audio 1") [official].
- Voice references: describe the voice in words as well [official].
- Notation: dialogue in quotes or `{}`, SFX in `<>`, music in `()`; "Character's line (emotion):
  content"; name the language before the line [official]. Production used labelled quoted lines
  [observed]. **[policy]** labelled lines: `[2–5s] @NAME (on camera, language + accent, delivery): "…"`.
- Unspecified language/music tends toward Mandarin speech and Chinese-sounding music — name the
  language and accent [reported].

## Multi-shot and timing

- Multiple shots with cuts inside one generation, from text: **yes**. Use timestamps or "Shot N"
  [official]. No dedicated multi-shot parameter for 2.5 on any platform [official]; production
  multi-shots were generated by text alone and cut where asked [observed].
- Timestamps: 1-second units, contiguous, no gaps; overstuffed ranges cause extra cuts or dropped
  beats; don't timestamp high-frequency actions [official].
- Transitions: give the moment and the method [official].
- **[policy]** `SHOT n (a–bs) — … HARD CUT.` / `BEAT n (a–bs)` for jump cuts inside a take; "cuts only
  at the specified points; the camera adds no cuts of its own".

## Negatives

- **No negative-prompt field** anywhere; exclusions live in the prompt text [official].
- Official guidance prefers positive descriptions and endorses negatives for subtitles and audio, yet
  its FAQ uses negatives for eyes ("normal human eyes; no glowing eyes") and duplicates ("do not
  generate duplicate versions of a character or a twin effect") [official].
- Production prompts ended with long, shot-specific NEGATIVE lists and held [observed].
- **[policy]** positive first, negative as fence: every predicted failure becomes a positive lock +
  a NEGATIVE item; never name a style you don't want primed if the token can leak (e.g. staging-map
  graphic vocabulary).

## Prompt structure and length

- Official 2.5 order: asset mapping → one-sentence summary → timeline / Shot N → notes on what stays
  constant [official]. The CINEORO spine follows it and adds locks, departments and a closing recap.
- Official length guidance: ≤1,000 English words [official]. Production prompts reached ~19,000
  characters (~3,000 words) on Higgsfield and were followed [observed]. **[policy]** full prompts
  usually land at 1,000–2,000 words of control text; above ~2,000 run the compression pass
  (`prompt-spine.md`); never add decoration.
- Camera vocabulary the model knows: extreme wide/wide/medium/medium close-up/close-up; push in, pull
  out, pan, track, follow, orbit, tilt, handheld shake; low angle, overhead, first-person; one-shot,
  dolly zoom, FPV, bullet time, speed ramp; niche terms as "[term + descriptive explanation]"
  [official]. **[policy]** add FOV in degrees and operator physics (`cineoro-camera`).
- Official optimizer skill exists (`sd25-pe`) [official] — optional; CINEORO prompts don't need it.

## Draft, edit, extend

- **Draft:** a 480p preview; finalizing the same draft renders 1080p without resending prompt or assets
  (resending errors); draft IDs last 7 days [official]. **[policy]** draft every multi-shot generation
  first to check blocking, cuts and timing.
- **Edit:** timestamped partial edits ("from 4–6 seconds in Video 1 … leave the rest unchanged");
  adaptive ratio, model-chosen duration, input 4–30 s; wording must match the task type [official].
- **Extend** forward/backward 4–30 s, repeatable; **transition** between two clips; **return last
  frame** for chaining [official].

## Platform notes

- **Higgsfield:** model `seedance_2_5`; 4–30 s; 480p/720p/1080p; `bitrate_mode` high = better quality
  at the same credit cost; draft available; modes t2v / omni-reference / video edit / video extension;
  no seed; no genre parameter on 2.5 [official]. `@` mentions of uploads/Elements were stored as
  `<<<image_1>>>`, `<<<audio_1>>>`, `<<<element-id>>>` tokens [observed].
- **BytePlus:** `@Image 1`, `@Video 1`, `@Audio 1`. **fal:** `@Image1`. **Replicate:** `[Image1]`
  (480p/720p only) [official].
- **Higgsfield Cinema Studio** exposes structured camera, lens, light-rig and palette controls with the
  same duration/resolution limits; its underlying model is not named [unverified].

## Known failure modes (and where the fix lives)

Subtitles from per-word tone tags or repeated dialogue words → `cineoro-sound` · music despite a ban →
ban with synonyms top and bottom · glowing eyes from strong emotional words → neutral wording ·
identity drift → headshot + full body, key refs first · clones/twins → ≤4 referenced people +
no-duplicates · wrong ref-to-character mapping → upload order · lip-sync drifting late → short lines,
none in the last seconds · reference image overriding text positions → fix the image · fingerprint
textures → downscale refs · bubbling audio → remove water/echo words. Full table:
`cineoro-realism/references/diagnosis.md`.
