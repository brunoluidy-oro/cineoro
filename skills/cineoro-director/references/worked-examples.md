## Example 1 — "The coffee before the bus" (scene types A + G)

**Header**
```
WHAT HAPPENS: Before dawn, a grandmother makes coffee in a cloth strainer for her grandson, who leaves
on the 6 a.m. bus; neither lets the goodbye become a goodbye.
WHAT IS SAID: LURDES: "Leva a garrafa. O café de lá não presta." / CAIO: "Eu ligo quando chegar, vó."
TIMING: pour and drip, her line — 7s · his face, eating, watching her hands — 6s · the thermos, his
line, one touch, she turns away — 7s = 20s
HOW IT ENDS: hard cut mid-turn, her back to him, her face never seen breaking.
```

```

text.
```
