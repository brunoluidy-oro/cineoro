## The "don't name it — describe its geometry" method

Some words pull a cartoon or a stock version of an object (in production, the word *bone* pulled a
cartoon chew bone with knobs). When an object keeps coming out wrong:
1. Remove its name from every block.
2. Describe its **geometry** (shape, taper, ends, curvature), **scale against a person** ("three to
   five metres, taller than a standing person"), **material and surface** ("weathered grey, pitted,
   lichen in the cracks") and **how it sits in the world** ("sweeping up out of the snow in a shallow
   arc, thickest where it enters the snow").
3. Negate the wrong versions explicitly ("no knob, no ball, no joint, no widening at either end;
   cartoon bone, chew bone, dumbbell shape, museum exhibit, aligned rows").
