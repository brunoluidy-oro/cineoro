# Bible interview

Ask in the user's language, a few questions at a time, offering a default for each so the user can
answer "ok". Skip what the material already answers. Stop asking when the decisions below are made.

## 1 · The film
- Logline (one or two sentences). Title and its meaning, if any.
- Format: feature / short / series episode / ad / music video / social.
- Runtime ceiling. Aspect ratio (default 16:9; 21:9 for monumental landscape; 9:16 for social).
- Platform and model (default: Higgsfield · Seedance 2.5 · 1080p · audio on).
- What must the audience believe? (the believability target — usually behaviour, not beauty)

## 2 · The world
- Place and period. Real culture, composite, or invented?
- What exists and what doesn't (materials, technology, objects).
- Daily life that must be right: how people make fire/food/shelter, travel, work, carry things.
- Objects that carry more than themselves (a knife, a ring, a letter).

## 3 · The people
- Each character: name, age, look, hands, temperament, what they want across the film
  (super-objective), what they never do.
- Who speaks, in which language and accent. Any invented language?
- Animals and their role.

## 4 · The look
- Light system: natural-only? What is the permanent state, what is the rare man-made exception, what
  colour appears only once?
- Key level per type of scene (low-key nights, mid-key days).
- Lens look (anamorphic / spherical large-format / 16mm / modern digital / phone).
- Reference films or photographers (optional — used only as anchors next to observable descriptions).

## 5 · The camera and the cut
- Who is the camera: an observer walking alongside, an author composing, a character?
- Handheld or composed by default; when does it switch?
- Editing grammar: jump cuts, long takes, alternating singles, composed key moments.
- Rules: low angle when…, canted horizon only when…, never…

## 6 · The sound
- Score or no score? If none: what replaces it (wind, breath, a drum, a lullaby)?
- Where silence is used.
- Voice continuity: audio references available?

## 7 · Structure and motifs
- Acts/blocks and their events.
- Motifs and rhymes: a gesture first and last, a line that changes meaning, a loop, a colour once.
- Scenes that must never be cut; scenes that compress into inserts.

## 8 · Assets
- What already exists (faces, turnarounds, plates, props, voice clips)? What must be built?
- Naming preferences for tags.

Defaults if the user wants to move fast: 16:9 · 1080p · Seedance 2.5 · audio on · natural motivated
light · spherical large-format look · observer handheld with authored key moments · no score ·
dialogue in the characters' native language.
