## 2. Places the skill pushed me AWAY from the request
- **The whole tree is aimed at live-action believability.** The description says "reads as believable live-action, not AI". The CONTRACT template opens with "Live-action photoreal film footage". The user wants claymation. I replaced it with "Handmade stop-motion CLAYMATION film footage" and turned "believability" into stop-motion craft: physical miniature set, fingerprints, boil.
- **Music ban.** CONTRACT, SOUND law 6, the spine, the standing NEGATIVE, and the ORDER recap all ban music with synonyms (melody, instrumental, chimes...). The user explicitly asked for "musiquinha alegre de fundo". I dropped the ban entirely, specified the music (instrumental, low, under the Foley), and kept only bans on vocals and narration. The realism spine does allow removing what the shot wants ("music in a film with a score"), but the CONTRACT template and the director checklist ("Music and subtitles banned at the top and the end?") don't say this.
- **"DIEGETIC ONLY, dirty-real" sound.** The user asked for caricature sounds. I wrote cartoon Foley (glop, boing, splat), which is not diegetic-real.
- **The camera laws push handheld, a breathing operator, and "never static/tripod"**, and the spine NEGATIVE bans tripods, locked cameras, and static frames. Stop-motion is shot locked-off. I used a locked miniature tripod and banned handheld instead.
- **"real-time motion" in CONTRACT, plus "Nothing floats, nothing loops" and smooth physicality.** The user wants jerky, stepped motion. I made "on twos" stepped motion the #1 lock and banned smooth, interpolated motion.
- **Standing NEGATIVE: plastic, waxy, smooth skin, doll face, CGI, 3D render, porcelain, clean.** "Plastic" fights plasticine. I kept CGI/3D/glossy-render bans but removed "plastic skin", "doll face", and "waxy", and asked for clay instead.
- **FILM LOOK: "MEGA-REAL, NOT a render", 35mm grain, low-key, defended darkness, chiaroscuro.** Claymation wants a cosy, readable mid-key. I used a warm mid-key miniature-studio look with soft fill and no heavy grain.
- **LIVING EYES physiology (saccades, pupils, asymmetry).** This doesn't apply to bead eyes. I rewrote it as stepped repositioning plus a replacement-lid blink.
- **The ACTING template (event/motive/obstacle) is heavy for a comic 10s puppet insert.** I used a compact version.
- **Header and card language.** The skill says to write them in the user's language, so they are in Portuguese. The prompt is in English, as the skill requires.
