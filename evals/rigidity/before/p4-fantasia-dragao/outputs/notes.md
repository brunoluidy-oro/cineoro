## 2. Where the skill pushed AWAY from the user's request
- **Music.** The user asked for "trilha orquestral épica crescendo". The director's CONTRACT says "NO music" always, sound law 6 calls for a music ban with synonyms at the top and the end, and the realism spine lists music/score/BGM. The fallback in the director table reads "music banned". Only realism says "Remove from the spine anything the shot actually wants (… music in a film with a score)". What I did: I followed the user. I put the score in the CONTRACT, in SOUND (building with the crane, sitting under the effects), and in ORDER. I banned only other music (synth, drone, chimes). On the card I mentioned adding the score in post as an alternative.
- **Glowing eyes.** The user asked for "olhos brilham". The realism standing spine has "glowing eyes" as a negative, and performance treats a fixed glow and stare as a failure. What I did: I kept the glowing eyes on the dragon, took "glowing eyes" out of the negative, and limited "glassy stare" to the warrior. I made the glow a light source that lights the scales, which fits light law 1.
- **Blue magic fire.** Light law 1 says "every light has a source that could exist in this world". The palette guidance and film-look template assume warm against cool. What I did: I treated the magic fire as a real in-world source with a Kelvin, a reach and mass, and made the palette cold-only, with no warm tones.
- **Lightning everywhere ("raios iluminam tudo").** "Say what stays dark", the single-key preference and "lit side never flips" all push against frame-wide flashes. What I did: I kept the flashes, put them at 3, 12 and 17s, and made them all come from frame-RIGHT so they don't break lit-side continuity. Darkness holds between flashes. This is a slight softening of "tudo".
- **Crane up.** The camera dept's operator-body doctrine, and the spine negatives "drone"/"gimbal"/"stabilized"/"floating", lean against a smooth rising move. What I did: I named it a real crane with mass, allowed it only in shot 4, kept the drone and gimbal negatives, and removed nothing the user asked for.
- **Realism ethos vs epic fantasy.** "Realism is what you subtract" and "MEGA-REAL / not CGI" fight a dragon in a video-game-style setup. I kept the fantasy and grounded it as live-action footage, which seems to be what the tree is for.
- **Level horizon in the final reveal.** That's a choice, not a conflict.
