# Scene types — starting skeletons

> **Calibration, not templates.** These skeletons show what each scene type tends to need and
> where it fails. Their camera/light/sound choices assume the realist preset — when the STYLE INTENT
> differs, keep the failure warnings and replace the choices.


Copy the closest type, then let the departments specialize it. Each entry: structure · camera · light
· locks · negatives to add · pitfall. Generalized from a production that found six reference prompts
covered ~90% of a film's shots; extended for ads and social formats.

**Contents:** A quiet intimate · B implied violence · C altered state · D crowd confrontation ·
E sensitive, partly hidden · F extreme wide with scale · G physical process · H alternating-singles
dialogue · I pursuit / action burst · J solitary interior with off-screen voice · K animal-centric ·
L product in use (ad) · M vertical social / UGC

---

## A · Quiet intimate (two people, a practical light)

- **Structure:** one take 8–15 s, or 2–3 shots; long pauses; the event is in a held moment.
- **Camera:** STAYS PUT and breathes; close-medium two-shot, low or at their level; 84° intimate wide
  or 29° for singles; level horizon (calm).
- **Light:** one small practical below or beside frame; faces lit from one side; darkness beyond 2 m.
- **Locks:** who touches whom and how; the listener's task; nobody cries aloud.
- **Negatives:** hysteria, melodrama, fast motion, tilted horizon, a kiss (if not), warm fill on faces.
- **Pitfall:** the engine resolves the tension with a hug or tears — lock the tactic of holding still.

## B · Implied violence (off frame)

- **Structure:** the hand enters, the movement begins, then the frame ends or holds the face/reaction
  while the hand leaves past the bottom edge. 6–12 s.
- **Camera:** close on the face or the hand; never tilts down to reveal.
- **Locks:** "ACTION BELOW FRAME" / "one short press, NO blood, NO gore" in every block that could show it.
- **Negatives:** visible blade entering, visible contact, wound, bright red blood, spurting, pooling,
  gore, splatter, camera tilting down, slow-motion violence.
- **Pitfall:** a reframe "helpfully" reveals the act — repeat the hidden-action lock in CAMERA.

## C · Altered state (madness, fever, panic, intoxication)

- **Structure:** 2–4 shots or one take; the state is a series of concrete tactics.
- **Camera:** shakes and jumps but STAYS PUT; canted horizon; no orbit.
- **Acting:** active eyes (darting or wide with sclera and tremor) or "absent competence".
- **Negatives:** orbiting camera, doll stare, calm face (if panic), lucid (if absent), maniacal laugh,
  villain grin, glowing eyes.
- **Pitfall:** "crazy" adjectives produce a horror mask — write markers and tactics.

## D · Crowd confrontation (multi-shot)

- **Structure:** 4–6 shots, 20–30 s; alternating leads with the crowd as a jury; the reversal on a face.
- **Camera:** low angle on the aggressor, high angle on the victim; same side of the axis; handheld.
- **Locks:** WHO IS WHO with sides and NEVER lists; anti-clone; populated background in every shot;
  contact map; weapon aim pinned to anatomy.
- **Negatives:** flat crowd, sharp background faces, people looking at camera, clones, the lead's face
  on an extra, empty background, modern clothes/weapons.
- **Pitfall:** >4 referenced identities — keep the crowd unreferenced, distinct, darker.

## E · Sensitive moment, action partly hidden

- **Structure:** one take or 2 shots; the meaningful action happens just out of frame or behind an
  object/body.
- **Locks:** "FACE NOT SHOWN, hands only" or "ACTION BELOW FRAME" in **every** block.
- **Negatives:** camera tilting down, reframing to reveal, close-up of the act.
- **Pitfall:** a single mention is ignored — repeat it in four places.

## F · Extreme wide with scale (and a focus rack)

- **Structure:** one shot 6–12 s; a small figure in a vast space; optionally a rack from a foreground
  detail to the figure.
- **Camera:** 8° from far (compression) or 107° low (expansion); stated distance in metres.
- **Locks:** distance and scale repeated in every block ("the figure occupies less than a tenth of the
  frame height").
- **Negatives:** subject too close, medium shot, drone, aerial, crane (unless wanted).
- **Pitfall:** the engine walks the camera closer — negate "medium shot".

## G · Physical process (craft, daily life)

- **Structure:** one take 12–20 s with BEATS, or 3–5 jump cuts; already in progress; full process with
  resistance, failure, retry, result, test.
- **Camera:** close on the hands, then travelling with the person; focus on the work.
- **Locks:** which hand does what; tools and their functions; nothing appears from nowhere.
- **Negatives:** effortless, one perfect smooth action, modern tools, objects appearing, cut to the
  result.
- **Pitfall:** the engine skips to the result — make each step a beat with seconds.

## H · Alternating-singles dialogue (shot / reverse)

- **Structure:** 3–6 shots, 15–28 s; each shot one speaker or one reaction; no wide unless needed.
- **Camera:** 29° or 18° singles, same side of the axis, eyelines matched (A looks frame-right, B
  frame-left).
- **Locks:** who speaks in which shot; listener mouths shut; eyelines; the lit side per character.
- **Negatives:** both speaking at once, wrong speaker lip-synced, crossing the axis, subtitles.
- **Pitfall:** long lines drift out of sync — split lines across shots.

## I · Pursuit / action burst

- **Structure:** 4–6 shots, 15–30 s; one action per shot; whole-second ranges; impacts on the cuts.
- **Camera:** walking/running handheld, jolts on impact, low angles; one movement per shot.
- **Locks:** screen direction (chaser and chased move frame-left to frame-right throughout); prop hands.
- **Negatives:** floaty jumps, weightless falls, stunt choreography, slow motion (unless chosen),
  drone chase.
- **Pitfall:** too many actions per range → dropped beats; keep one action per shot.

## J · Solitary interior with an off-screen voice

- **Structure:** 4–6 jump-cut beats, 15–25 s; one body, one spot, one orientation.
- **Locks:** orientation never flips; the camera stays on one side of the body axis; her mouth stays
  shut; the voice belongs to nobody in frame.
- **Negatives:** her lips moving, a figure at the door, orientation flipping, warm light (if cold).
- **Pitfall:** the engine lip-syncs the visible face — repeat the mouth-shut lock in DIALOGUE, LOCKS,
  NEGATIVE.

## K · Animal-centric

- **Structure:** one take or 2–3 shots; the animal's concrete tactic (fight, hold the line, watch the
  handler).
- **Camera:** at the animal's level; tele from distance for wild animals.
- **Negatives:** glowing, magical, cartoon, stuffed-toy, plastic fur, looking into the lens, tame (if
  wild), a pack (if one).
- **Pitfall:** anthropomorphic expressions — write ears, nostrils, weight, tail.

## L · Product in use (advertising)

- **Structure:** 8–20 s, 2–5 shots; the product is *used* by a person with a real task, not presented.
  Hero detail shot as an insert with information (texture, mechanism), not decoration.
- **Camera:** observational 47–63° for use; 29°/12° for the detail; a final hold on the product in
  context.
- **Light:** motivated (window, practical); the product's material defined by the light (speculars on
  metal, translucency on liquid).
- **Locks:** the product's exact geometry, colour and logo placement restated in words (logos and text
  are fragile — supply them as a reference image; avoid generated text); which hand holds it.
- **Negatives:** floating product, glossy CGI packshot, warped logo, garbled text, smiling at the lens
  (unless the brief wants a direct address), stock-footage gestures.
- **Pitfall:** "beautiful product shot" produces CGI gloss — write material, light and use.

## M · Vertical social / UGC

- **Structure:** 9:16, 6–20 s; first second already in action (scroll-stop); one person owns the frame.
- **Camera:** phone-real handheld or a held selfie at arm's length; 63–84°; deep focus.
- **Look:** phone capture look (`cineoro-camera` format F); auto-exposure breathing; mixed white balance.
- **Sound:** direct speech to camera allowed; room tone; no music unless the format wants it.
- **Negatives:** cinematic shallow focus, colour grade, anamorphic, studio light, wide 21:9 framing.
- **Pitfall:** the engine makes it "cinematic" — say "phone footage" in CONTRACT and FILM LOOK.
