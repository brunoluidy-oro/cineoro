# Film locks and light blocks — paste-ready by world

Each entry gives a LIGHT block and the colour/exposure core of a FILM LOOK. Adapt the brackets, then
freeze the result in the project bible and paste it verbatim from then on. Add the lens paragraph from
`cineoro-camera/references/format-and-lens-looks.md`.

**Contents:** 1 night by fire · 2 single practical interior (lamp/candle) · 3 blue dusk / polar
twilight (mid-key) · 4 overcast day · 5 harsh midday sun · 6 interior by window · 7 urban night ·
8 moonlight · 9 burning interior (fire as the scene) · 10 dawn grey, cold ruin · 11 light that is
born or changes during the take

## 1 · Night by fire (low-key)

```
LIGHT — NATURAL LIGHT ONLY, night, DIM & COLD (KEY): the only key is the campfire off-screen
[frame-left], low, ~1900 K; faint cold sky ambience ~10000 K on the ground and the far shapes. The
fire paints a restless warm flicker on the camera-facing side of every face and dies within three
metres; beyond it, cold blue-black. Exposed for the fire-lit faces; the far side of each face falls
into deep shadow. Shadows deep but breathing, never crushed; even exposure, NO vignette. The fire's
side never flips between shots. NOT: daylight, flat even light, a second warm source, beauty fill,
milky blacks.
FILM LOOK core: real 65mm film, 500-speed tungsten stock pushed; fire-amber against deep indigo;
warm amber halation on skin and fur, cool halation on breath; highlights bloom and roll off; heavy
fine moving grain in the dark; dense, tack-defined real photographic detail on faces and hands.
```

## 2 · Single practical interior (oil lamp, candle, stone lamp)

```
LIGHT — the only light is the small flame of [the lamp] on [the floor / the table] [below frame
edge], ~1800 K. It lights the UNDERSIDE of their faces only and dies within a metre; the room behind
them is black. The flame bends and recovers with each draught and the light on the faces breathes
with it. Strong chiaroscuro, low-key. NOT: bright light, ceiling light, window light, flat fill,
warm light on everything, uplight glow.
```

## 3 · Blue dusk / polar twilight (MID-KEY)

```
LIGHT — NATURAL LIGHT ONLY, LOW RAKING TWILIGHT, NO HARD SHADOWS (KEY): one weak low source near the
horizon on [frame-right] lays a warm edge along fur, hair, wood and the crests of the snow; everything
turned away sits in cool cyan-blue; soft, hazy, shadowless. The glow stays low on the same side of
the horizon in every shot. NOT: sun disc in frame, sunbeams, moon, aurora, orange or pink sky,
golden-hour postcard light, hard shadows.
FILM LOOK core: real photochemical negative, softly printed, MID-KEY — NOT underexposed, NOT dark;
two temperatures — cool desaturated cyan-teal sky and shadowed snow against warm tan-ochre materials
and skin; muted, never teal-and-orange; LIFTED WARM shadows, absolutely NO crushed blacks; highlights
roll off gently and BLOOM with halation around the brightest snow; low micro-contrast, slightly hazy
like cold air on an old print; heavy moving grain most visible in the sky; exposure even corner to
corner, NO vignette.
```

## 4 · Overcast day (soft, documentary)

```
LIGHT — NATURAL DAYLIGHT, OVERCAST, ~6500 K: a large soft top-front sky source; gentle modelling
from above, darker eye sockets, soft shadow under chin and brows; no hard shadows. Background slightly
brighter than the faces; exposed for skin. NOT: sun, sunbeams, lens flare, glossy speculars, HDR,
saturated sky.
FILM LOOK core: muted natural colour, slightly cool, soft contrast, fine grain, realistic skin with
pores and blemishes.
```

## 5 · Harsh midday sun (drought, desert, white sky)

```
LIGHT — NATURAL LIGHT ONLY, HARD MIDDAY SUN, DRY & WHITE (KEY):
SOURCE: the sun ~5600 K high and slightly behind [frame-left]; a pale washed-out sky with no clouds
acting as a weak cool top fill; the bare ground as the only bounce, warm and dusty.
REACH: short hard shadows under brows, nose and chin; hat brims throw a hard band of shadow across
the eyes (the eyes read only in the bounce); hot specular highlights on sweat and leather.
EXPOSURE: exposed for skin in the bounce; the sky allowed to go near-white but holding a trace of
tone; highlights roll off, never clipped flat; shadows dense but detailed; even exposure, NO
vignette.
CONTINUITY: the sun stays on the same side for the same time of day across all shots of the scene;
lit sides never flip.
NOT: soft beauty light, reflector fill, golden hour, saturated blue sky, clouds (if drought), HDR,
flattened contrast, lens flare streaks.
```
Note: with the sun near frame, anamorphic looks pull streak flares — prefer a spherical lens look, or
keep the sun well out of frame and fence flares.

## 6 · Interior by window

```
LIGHT — the only key is the window on [frame-right], cool daylight ~6000 K, falling across her face
from the side; the room behind falls off into warm brown shadow; dust in the beam. The camera sits on
the shadow side; half of her face is in shadow. NOT: ceiling lights, frontal fill, even exposure of
the whole room, blown-out window that floods the frame.
```

## 7 · Urban night (mixed practicals)

```
LIGHT — MOTIVATED PRACTICALS ONLY: a sodium street lamp behind [frame-left] ~2000 K as the rim and
key; a cold fluorescent shop sign off-screen right ~4500 K as a green-cyan fill on the other side of
the face; wet asphalt reflecting both. Between sources, darkness. NOT: neon overload, cyberpunk
colour, purple haze, glossy wet look on everything, flat streetlight wash.
```

## 8 · Moonlight

```
LIGHT — MOONLIGHT ONLY: a cold, weak top-back light ~7000 K, silver edges on hair and shoulders,
faces mostly in soft shadow with only the eyes catching a glint; the landscape readable in low
contrast; no colour except a faint blue. NOT: day-for-night bright blue wash, visible moon disc
blowing out, flat even exposure.
```

## 9 · Burning interior (fire is the scene)

```
LIGHT: NIGHT, fire the only source — big restless orange masses against black inside, sparks and
smoke; his face always painted in warm flicker, the wet eyes catching the flames; outside the
doorway, cold blue-black. FILM LOOK core: subtle fine moving grain, strong halation on flames,
shallow focus riding the face, smoke blooming through the light; whites hot but held, blacks deep and
oily. NOT: CGI flames, cartoon fire, looping fire, explosion, lens flare.
```

## 10 · Dawn grey, cold ruin (nothing burns)

```
LIGHT: early grey DAWN, COLD ONLY — blue-grey light down through the broken roof and through torn
walls, soft and shadowless; NO fire, NO embers, NO warm glow anywhere; faces lit grey, skin matte.
DARK, muted frame. FILM LOOK core: whites suppressed, blacks deep and soft, razor-shallow focus on
the face, halation on backlit lashes and the sky hole. NOT: fire, embers, glowing coals, warm glow,
orange light, smoke, sunbeams, golden light.
```

## 11 · Light that is born or changes during the take

A fire being lit, a lamp switched on, a door opened onto sun, a car's headlights arriving. The
engine's instinct is to show the end state from frame one (the fire already burning) and to pre-light
the dark part with an invisible studio key. Write the light as **START / DURING / END with seconds**,
tie every change to the physical event, and lock that nothing glows before its cause.

```
LIGHT — MOTIVATED LIGHT ONLY, [time], [two words] (KEY):
START (0–Ns): the only light is [the ambient that already exists: weak cold moonlight from behind
frame-right, ~8000 K] — [the subject] dim but readable; [brief flashes from the event, e.g. each spark
shower lights her hands for a split second].
DURING (N–Ms): [the new source is born: a coin-sized ember, ~1800 K, a tiny orange glow on fingertips
and chin, brightening with each breath and dulling between].
END (M–Ts): [the new source is now the key: the small flame low in front of her, ~1900 K, flickering
on the underside of her face; it reaches 2–3 m and dies].
EXPOSURE: exposed for the subject in whatever light exists each second; the frame brightens ONLY
because the source grows — never by an invisible lamp. Shadows breathing, NO vignette.
LOCK: no glow, flame or [lamp light] before Ns — WRONG if the end-state light exists in frame one.
NOT: studio light, frontal fill, headlamp, a bright moon, warm light before the source exists.
```
Repeat the "nothing before Ns" lock in ABSOLUTE LOCKS, in the timeline beats, in NEGATIVE and in
ORDER — it is one of the critical few.
